# Notch and menu bar

Nora keeps you informed without the chat window in front.

## The notch

On a MacBook with a notch, turn on **Enable notch control** in **[Settings › General](../settings/general.md)**. The notch then shows the agent's running tasks and their progress, and asks you there when something needs approval, such as starting computer use.

## The menu bar

**Show menu bar icon** in **Settings › General** adds Nora to the menu bar, with quick access to the chat, Settings and **Check for Updates…**.

## The Dock

**Show dock icon** chooses whether Nora appears in the Dock. Dock changes take effect after you restart Nora.
