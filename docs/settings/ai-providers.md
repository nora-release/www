# AI Providers

Every provider account Nora can use, with its usage. Models run through `nora-gateway`, the model gateway on your Mac.

## Add a provider

Click **Add Provider**. The sheet has three tabs:

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts | Sign in with a subscription through a provider plugin: **Sign In with Browser**, **Add Account** for a second account, or **Add Key** where a plugin also takes keys. |
| Preset | Search known providers and paste an **API Key**. |
| Custom | Add any service with its **Name**, **Base URL**, **API format** (OpenAI Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses or Anthropic Messages), **API Key** and, optionally, **Models**. |

Provider plugins themselves are installed on the Plugins page. See [subscription sign-ins](../providers/sign-ins.md).

## Provider cards

Each account appears as a card with its usage. Plans with limits show how much of the current window you have used. Some providers offer a daily **Check In**.

## Models

Open a provider's models to choose which appear in the composer. **Search models**, turn each on or off, or use **Turn All On** / **Turn All Off**. **Refresh** asks the provider for its current list.

## Remove an account

Click **Remove** on the account. Conversations that used it are kept.
