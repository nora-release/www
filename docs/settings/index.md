# Settings overview

Nora's settings live in one window. Open it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>, from the Nora menu, or from the menu bar icon.

## How saving works

Each Settings page is edited as a draft. Change what you like, then click **Save** (or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>) to apply it, or **Cancel** to leave everything as it was. Nothing changes until you save.
